# VeriWorkly Studio — Pricing & Entitlements Model

## Subscription Plans
- **Free Tier**: Local storage, document builder, Master Profile store, 6 resume templates, local exports (PDF/DOCX/HTML/MD/JSON), free ATS scans.
- **3-Day Sprint Pass ($2.99)**: 3-day Creator Pro subdomain hosting + 150 AI writing credits.
- **7-Day Hunt Pass ($5.99)**: 7-day Creator Pro subdomain hosting + 400 AI writing credits.
- **Monthly Pro ($14.99/mo)**: Creator Pro subdomain hosting + 1,000 monthly AI credits + 300 ATS scans/period.
- **Annual Pro ($11.99/mo eq, $143.88/yr)**: All Monthly Pro features with 20% annual discount.

## Credit Packs (Top-Ups)
- **250 AI Credits**: $2.99 (90 days validity)
- **500 AI Credits**: $4.99 (90 days validity)
